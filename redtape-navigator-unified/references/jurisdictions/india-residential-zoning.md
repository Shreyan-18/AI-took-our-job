# Offline Lead Snapshot: Residential Zoning & Building Permits (UNVERIFIED FALLBACK)

> Verify local municipal building bylaws, FAR, and setback rules live (`source_verified_at: null` if used offline).

| Item | Tier | Lead Criteria (Verify Live) | Portal to Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building Plan Sanction / Extension Permit | Municipal | Required for new construction or structural floor additions; depends on plot area, FAR, setbacks, road width | City Municipal / Development Authority portal (e.g., KMC, KMDA, DDA, BBMP) |
| Land Use / Zoning Clearance (LUDCP / Master Plan) | Municipal/State | Verifies residential vs mixed-use zoning and heritage/eco-sensitive overlays | State Urban Development / Municipal Planning portal |
| Mutation & Property Tax Clearance | Municipal | Proof of recorded ownership and zero municipal tax arrears | Municipal Property Tax portal |
| Completion / Occupancy Certificate | Municipal | Post-construction sign-off before occupancy | Municipal Building Dept portal |
| Supporting Docs (Typical) | — | Registered sale deed/patta, mutation certificate, tax receipt, licensed architect/LBS structural drawing, soil test (for G+2/G+3+), neighbour NOC if applicable | — |
